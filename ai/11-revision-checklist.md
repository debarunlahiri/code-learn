# Revision Checklist

## Around 3 years

- Explain bias, variance, leakage, regularization, and cross-validation.
- Choose metrics for imbalance and unequal error costs.
- Compare linear models, trees, ensembles, clustering, and neural networks.
- Build leakage-safe scikit-learn pipelines.
- Use pandas, NumPy, SQL joins, aggregations, and window functions.
- Package a model behind a validated API.
- Explain experiment tracking, artifact versioning, drift, and rollback.
- Describe embeddings, vector retrieval, chunking, and grounded generation.
- Prepare three project stories with measurable impact and one failure lesson each.

## Around 6 years

- Translate product outcomes into objectives, guardrails, and evaluation strategy.
- Design batch, online, streaming, recommendation, fraud, and RAG systems.
- Reason quantitatively about QPS, tail latency, compute, storage, and cost.
- Handle feedback loops, delayed labels, drift, fairness, and governance.
- Define safe rollout, rollback, retraining, and incident-response processes.
- Evaluate build-versus-buy decisions and platform trade-offs.
- Explain prioritization, technical leadership, mentoring, and stakeholder alignment.
- Present architecture decisions with assumptions and rejected alternatives.

## Final self-check

- Can every metric be connected to user or business cost?
- Can every architecture survive missing data, dependency failure, and rollback?
- Are training and live features point-in-time consistent?
- Are access control and sensitive-data handling explicit?
- Are model, data, code, and configuration versions traceable?
- Is there a simple baseline and a reason for added complexity?
- Are answers supported by specific project evidence?
